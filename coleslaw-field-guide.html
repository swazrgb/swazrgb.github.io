Wiki

Coleslaw vs Uranium

Both worlds run the same OpenRSC server. Uranium is configured to play like authentic RSC; Coleslaw switches on the custom content. Each page below covers one difference: what it does, where to find it, the numbers, and why you'd care.

Almost everything comes from three kinds of setting:

location_data: 2 / 1

Loads the extra spawn files: auction clerks, runecraft altars, harvest patches, custom quest NPCs, shortcuts.

restrict_item_id: -1 / 1289

Uranium refuses every item above ID 1289, so all 303 custom items (and scenery above 1188) can't exist there.

~80 feature flags

Everything from batching and 2× XP to the equipment tab and hidden roofs.

Every page starts with its settings: Coleslaw value then Uranium value. Facts come from reading the server code; anything I couldn't confirm is marked unverified. XP figures are the displayed amounts before Coleslaw's 2× rate unless a page says otherwise.

  • 2× experienceDouble XP for combat and skills, including quest rewards.
  • Faster game tick430 ms ticks instead of 640 ms: the whole world runs about 1.5× faster.

Short members quest that unlocks Runecraft.

SettingColeslawUranium

Start: the Duke of Lumbridge, upstairs in Lumbridge Castle. Requirements: none, just a free inventory slot.

  1. The Duke gives you an Air talisman.

  3. Give the package to Aubury in Varrock. He gives you Research notes and offers a free teleport back to the Wizards' Tower.

  • access to Runecraft
  • Sedridor and Aubury will now teleport you to the rune stone mine

Why it's useful

It is the only way into Runecraft, and it takes a few minutes with no items needed.

Peeling the Onion

Free quest: become an ogre to help Kresh. Cooking and Crafting XP.

SettingColeslawUranium
want_custom_queststruefalse

Start: Kresh the ogre in his house in Lumbridge Swamp, or Wizard Sedridor ("Do you have anything you need doing?"). Requirements: Crafting 5.

  1. Talk to Kresh and Sedridor. Sedridor suggests making you an ogre; you can say no and come back later.
  2. Visit the Make over mage (north-west of the Crafting Guild). He gives you a free Makeover Waiver; read it and sign.
  3. Back at the mage, he turns you into an ogre. This is a real appearance change: ogre skin, face and hair.
  4. Aggie in Draynor makes Soft Yellowgreen Clay from 1 soft clay, 4 onions and 1 woad leaf.
  5. "Shape" the clay into Ogre Ears (Crafting 5).
  6. Use a knife on leather armour to make a Leather vest.
  7. Wear the ears and vest and talk to Kresh.
  8. Search the bookcase in the Wizards' Tower basement for the Ogre recipes and give them to Kresh.

Rewards

  • 2 quest points
  • Cooking XP of 50 + 25 per Cooking level, and Crafting XP of 50 + 18.75 per Crafting level (doubled by the 2× rate)
  • 750 gp from Sedridor
  • one free makeover (normally 3,000 gp)
  • new skin colours: yellow, zombie, purple and "Kresh green"

Afterwards Aggie will make replacement clay for 20 gp plus materials.

Why it's useful

Short, free-to-play, and the XP grows with your level, so doing it late pays more.

Runecraft

A new skill: mine rune stones, bind them into runes at hidden altars.

SettingColeslawUranium
want_runecrafttruefalse

Runecraft is a whole extra skill. You mine rune stones, carry them to an altar behind a set of Mysterious Ruins, and turn them into runes. XP figures below are displayed XP before the 2× rate.

Unlocking it

Complete the Rune Mysteries quest. Every Runecraft action checks for it.

Rune stones

  • After the quest, Wizard Sedridor (Wizards' Tower basement) and Aubury (Varrock rune shop) can teleport you to the rune stone mine, a separate area in the far north of the map.
  • The mine is one Raw Rune stone rock that never runs out. Any pickaxe works, there is no Mining level requirement, every swing succeeds, and each stone gives 5 Mining XP.
  • With batching, one click fills your pack at about a stone per tick.
  • The exit portal takes you back to whoever sent you.
  • Mortimer the certer accepts rune stones.

Altars

Enter the Mysterious Ruins with the matching talisman (click Enter, or use the talisman on the ruins). Inside, click Bind on the altar: one click converts every rune stone you carry. A talisman's Locate option tells you which direction its ruins are.

In practice Runecraft stops at Nature. Both entering the ruins and binding at the altar need the matching talisman in your pack, and no player can get a Law, Death or Blood talisman:

  • Imbuing them is commented out in the code as a TODO.
  • No drop table, shop, quest, ground spawn or NPC gives one.
  • Curse and Enfeeble only convert a talisman you already have.

The Law ruins do stand on Entrana, so Law is blocked only by the missing talisman. Death and Blood are blocked twice: their ruins aren't placed anywhere in the world, so there's no entrance outside. Their altar rooms exist, but their exit portals point to 0,0, and the Death ruins' teleport is a copy of Mind's.

The Water talisman's Locate points to the wrong place.

Talismans

  • Uncharged: chisel a rune stone (5 Crafting XP).
  • Normal: use an uncharged talisman with 10 runes of the type. You need the altar's level. Ten runes from a shop are enough to unlock an altar. Normal talismans are reusable.
  • Cursed: cast Curse on a normal talisman. Needs the altar level +7. The bind gives 2× XP with normal runes, then the talisman crumbles.
  • Enfeebled: cast Enfeeble on a normal talisman. Needs the altar level +14. The bind gives 5× XP but no runes, deals 3 damage and drains 15% of your Runecraft level.
  • If you carry several, enfeebled is used first, then cursed. A stray enfeebled talisman costs you the whole load of runes.

Why it's useful

Free, unlimited runes for Magic, with no Mining level needed for the stones. Cosmic, chaos and nature runes are valuable (nature feeds High Alchemy). Cursed and enfeebled talismans trade runes for faster levels. See also Runecraft potions.

Harvesting

A gathering skill for fruit, vegetables, herbs and other farm produce.

SettingColeslawUranium
want_harvestingtruefalse

169 patches across the map: fruit trees, palms, berry bushes, vegetable allotments, sugar cane, grape vines, and clip spots for herbs, seaweed, limpwurt and snape grass. Nothing is planted. You walk up to a patch and harvest it, and it depletes and regrows like a tree.

Tools

All sold by the Gardener (see below).

Tool Used on Without it
Fruit Picker trees, palms, pineapple 50% chance per attempt to damage the produce
Hand Shovel everything else you Harvest 50% chance per attempt to damage the produce
Herb Clippers herb, seaweed, limpwurt, snape grass can't clip at all
Watering Can watering events (5 uses per fill) the plant is weak and that attempt fails
Bucket of soil soil events (fill at a Soil Mound) the plant is weak and that attempt fails

Care events

  • Each Harvest attempt has a 1 in 7 chance of asking for water; if not, a 1 in 5 chance of asking for soil. Having the item satisfies it and protects that patch for 3 minutes.
  • While a patch is watered, a harvest has a 1 in 21 chance not to deplete it.
  • While a patch has soil, a harvest has a 1 in 15 chance of an extra item.

Each grape costs 1 Prayer point. Doing it in the rival faction's area spawns a hostile monk. Blessed grapes on a jug of water make Wine of Saradomin (Cooking 70), an ingredient of the Magic potion.

Why it's useful

Herbs, limpwurt and snape grass without combat, which means Herblaw supplies on tap. It is also the only source of the ingredients for the new potions and foods, and it trains from level 1.

Wiki / Game rules

Batching

One click repeats a skilling action until the batch is done.

SettingColeslawUranium
batch_progressiontruefalse

On Uranium, like original RSC, one click is one attempt. You click a tree, swing once, get a log or "You slip and fail to hit the tree", and have to click again.

With batching on, one click starts a batch: the server repeats the attempt in a loop until the batch count runs out, something interrupts you, or the resource disappears. A progress bar with a Cancel button appears on screen.

How many attempts a batch gets

Gathering (woodcutting, mining, gem rocks, fishing, pickpocketing, stalls, firemaking, harvesting) scales with your base level in the skill, not your boosted or drained level:

Level ≤10 11–19 20–29 30–39 40–49 50–59 60–69 70–79 80–89 90–95 96–99
Attempts 10 12 14 16 20 24 32 40 48 56 64

Failed attempts count. A batch of 64 is 64 swings, not 64 logs.

Processing is limited by what you carry:

  • The number of input items: cooking, identifying herbs, spinning, burying bones, filling vessels.
  • The smaller of two ingredient stacks: mixing potions, stringing bows, smelting ore pairs.
  • Free inventory slots: picking flax or wheat, shearing sheep.
  • A fixed 5: cutting arrow shafts, arrow tips and similar fletching.
  • Smithing asks you: Make 1 / Make 5 / Make 10 / Make All. This menu only exists when batching is on.

Speed

Each attempt costs the skill's own delay plus a pause in the batch loop. The pause differs by skill, and that decides whether a batch is faster or slower than clicking once per attempt (as on Uranium):

Loop pause Skills Compared with clicking
none Mining, gem rocks, Harvesting, rune stones, shearing, sand, refilling, arrow fletching batch is faster
1 tick Woodcutting, fishing, cooking, smithing, most crafting, spinning the same
2 ticks Herblaw (identify, potions, grinding), pickpocketing, bow stringing, gem cutting, glass blowing, log cutting batch is slower

Clicking during a batch

Tested on a test server with batching on:

  • Clicking the action again mid-batch does nothing. While a batch runs you count as busy, and the server ignores action clicks. It doesn't restart or cancel the batch.
  • A walk click is the only way to cut a batch short. You don't move; the batch just stops after the attempt in progress.
  • A new action click is accepted on the tick after the batch's last result, so clicking as soon as you see it wastes no time.

For the 2-tick skills, alternating action → walk → action beats letting the batch run:

Test Plain batch Action → walk → action Uranium, clicking every tick
Identify 20 herbs 39 ticks 30 ticks 20 ticks
Pickpocketing 1 attempt per 3 ticks 1 per 2 ticks 1 per 2 ticks

What stops a batch

The batch ends after the current attempt when:

  • you move off your tile, or click to walk anywhere
  • you press Cancel on the progress bar
  • you are attacked, or start combat
  • someone sends you a trade request
  • the resource is gone: the tree falls, the rock empties, the fishing spot depletes
  • mining finds a gem
  • you run out of materials to process

A walk click while busy only cancels the batch. The walk itself is dropped, so you have to click a second time to actually move.

A full inventory does not stop gathering. Extra items drop to the ground with "Your Inventory is full, the X drops to the ground!"

Fatigue

On Uranium, fatigue was the natural brake on AFK skilling: at 100% you gain no XP and gathering refuses to start. Coleslaw has fatigue turned off, so nothing but the interrupts above ends a batch. That is why Coleslaw also turns on depleting fishing spots and extended mining rocks.
